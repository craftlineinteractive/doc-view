## 3. Full Description
Doc View is a fast, clean document manager and reader designed for Android. Open, read, and manage PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), TXT files, and images directly on your device.

Key Features:

DOCUMENT READING & VIEWING
- High-performance PDF reader with smooth vertical scrolling, multi-touch pinch zoom, and fast full-text keyword search.
- Clean Office document viewing for Word (DOCX), Excel spreadsheets (XLSX grid viewer), and PowerPoint presentations (PPTX).
- Clean text viewer for TXT files and full-screen image inspection.
- System light and dark theme support with true dark backgrounds.

AUTOMATIC ORGANIZATION & MANAGEMENT
- Automatic document indexing across your device storage to find documents instantly.
- Folder browser (Directories) to navigate files by storage paths.
- Built-in Recycle Bin: safely store deleted files for 30 days before permanent deletion, with one-tap restore.
- File management: rename files, view detailed file info, add bookmarks, and share via system share sheets.
- Recents list for quick access to your recently viewed files.

PRODUCTIVITY & PDF TOOLS
- Scan to PDF: capture paper documents with your camera and convert them into clean multi-page PDFs.
- Image to PDF: select multiple photos and convert them into a single PDF document.
- Create PDF: start a new blank or lined document with custom page layouts.
- Merge PDF: combine multiple PDF files into one structured document.
- Split PDF: extract custom page ranges or separate individual pages.
- Manage Pages: reorder, rotate, or delete pages in any PDF file.
- Annotate & Draw: highlight key passages, underline, add freehand ink drawings, and save annotations.
- Add Text: type custom text notes onto any PDF page with customizable fonts, colors, and sizing.
- Sign Documents: draw and store reusable signatures and place them anywhere on your documents.
- PDF to Word: extract text content from PDF files into standard DOCX format.
- Print: print documents directly to any Wi-Fi or cloud-connected printer.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS & NOTIFICATION
- Recent Files home screen widget (4x2): glance at your latest documents and open them with one tap.
- Quick Tools widget (4x1): quick shortcuts to Scan, Image to PDF, Search, and Import right from your home screen.
- Optional Quick Access notification: persistent shortcuts in your notification tray (disabled by default, easy toggle in Settings).

PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTION
Doc View is free to use with optional non-intrusive ads. Upgrade to Doc View Premium to remove all ads and unlock advanced tools:
- Ad-free reading across all screens
- Advanced PDF tools: Manage Pages, Annotate, Add Text, Sign, and PDF to Word
- Flexible subscription plans with eligible free trial periods managed directly through Google Play. Cancel anytime in your Google Play subscriptions settings.

PRIVACY & DATA INTEGRITY
Doc View processes all documents locally on your device. Your file contents, personal documents, and signatures remain strictly private and are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service.
